FutureGenerali
Lifecycle Copy & AI Chatbot

Website copy and AI chatbot conversational flows — designed in Figma, structured around pre-determined decision trees for a regulated insurance context.

ClientFutureGenerali India
SurfacesWeb copy · Chatbot flows
StatusLive

Scope

Full homepage MVP copy and product page copy (New Assured Wealth Plan) for the FutureGenerali website. Alongside this, UX microcopy and pre-determined conversational flow design for the AI chatbot — covering entry points, decision branches, fallback states, and handoff triggers.

Approach

Website copy was written to maintain brand voice and user journey coherence across a regulated financial services context, working directly within Figma wireframes from brief to developer handoff. The chatbot work required structuring conversation flows as decision logic, not prose — mapping user intent to response branches before any copy was written.

Deliverable

Production website copy (live); Figma-based chatbot flow documentation delivered to the development team.
