Figma UI/UX Microcopy

Interface microcopy for HDFC's enterprise fintech products — labels, states, and in-product flows written in Figma alongside the design team, with roughly 60% of recommendations shipping as written.

ClientHDFC · Enterprise fintech
DisciplineUI/UX microcopy
Adoption~60% shipped as written

Context

HDFC's product surfaces are high-stakes and high-volume — regulated financial flows where a single unclear label or error message costs the user real money or a failed transaction. The work was interface writing: the words that live inside the product, not the marketing around it.

Scope

UI/UX microcopy only — field labels, helper text, empty and error and success states, confirmations, and the copy that carries a user through a flow. Deliberately narrow: this engagement was pure interface writing, with no web copy and no CMS publishing, so every decision could be made at the level of the individual screen.

Approach

Microcopy is decided at the character level, in context. The work happened inside Figma, on the actual frames, alongside the designers — each label, state, and button mapped to what the user is trying to do at that exact moment. Error states were rewritten to say what to do next rather than what went wrong; CTAs were made to name both the action and its outcome; jargon was replaced with the words customers already use.

  • Written directly on the Figma frames, in the same file as the design — copy and interface resolved together.
  • Every state accounted for — default, empty, loading, error, success — not just the happy path.
  • Recommendations logged as annotations the product team could accept, adapt, or reject on the spot.
Impact
Roughly 60% of microcopy recommendations shipped as written.

Deliverable

Figma-annotated UI/UX microcopy handed to the product and design teams for implementation.

Note · HDFC's product screens are covered by client confidentiality, so this work is described rather than shown. No client assets are reproduced here.
