What it is

A full-stack content operations dashboard built to replace the spreadsheet systems most content teams rely on for delivery management. Role-based access, project tracking, status reporting, and administrative oversight — in a single interface.

The problem it solved

Content delivery at agency scale runs across multiple writers, multiple clients, and multiple brief cycles simultaneously. Visibility collapses without a system. This tool was built to give team leads and contributors a shared operational layer — without the overhead of a full project management platform.

How it works

Two access levels: Admin and Writer. Admins create article tasks, assign writers, set deadlines, and track delivery against a dashboard showing active projects, overdue tasks, per-writer workload, and monthly completions. Writers log in to view assigned work, update status, add notes, and submit final Google Doc links.

Articles move through a defined status flow — Brief Pending, Writing, Review, Revision, Completed — with a status log maintained at the database level. Search and filter capability is built in across all article views.
