Scope

Onboarding microcopy, in-app copy, and brand-led launch pages for Jupiter Money — a fintech neobank targeting young urban professionals.

Approach

Fintech microcopy demands precision at the character level. Every label, tooltip, and error state was written to reduce user friction without sacrificing regulatory clarity. Brand voice was maintained from onboarding through to transactional states — surfaces where most products abandon tone entirely.

Deliverable

Figma-reviewed microcopy and launch page copy; implemented by the Jupiter Money product team. Available in the portfolio.
