Trading Platform Copy

Product and platform page copy for Mastertrust, an Indian online trading platform — written to make a feature-dense product legible to retail traders without losing regulatory precision.

ClientMastertrust
SurfacesProduct · Platform pages
StatusLive

Context

Mastertrust is an online trading platform spanning equity, F&O, and commodities for Indian retail investors. Trading products are dense by nature — margins, order types, brokerage slabs, compliance disclosures — and the page copy has to make that legible to a first-time trader and a seasoned one on the same screen.

Scope

Product and platform page copy — the surfaces where a prospective trader decides whether the platform is fast enough, cheap enough, and trustworthy enough to move their account to.

Approach

Fintech copy at the trading layer is a precision exercise. Every claim about speed, pricing, or execution carries regulatory weight, so the work was to translate platform features into trader-facing benefits without overstating them — leading with the decision the trader is making, then backing it with the specifics a regulated financial product is required to state.

Deliverable

Production product and platform page copy; implemented live.
