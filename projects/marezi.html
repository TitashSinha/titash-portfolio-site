Marezi
AI-Driven Web Copywriting

The full website for Marezi (formerly Q-SYS), a B2B SaaS customer experience platform by Riana Group serving banks, hospitals, and restaurants across 15+ African countries — positioning, page structure, and copy, built page by page in Figma with the designer.

Scope Full site · 7 solution pages · Industry pages
Role Positioning · IA · Copy
Status Live

Context

Marezi (formerly Q-SYS) is an intelligent, data-driven customer experience platform developed by the Riana Group. It is deployed across high-volume environments — banks, hospitals, restaurants — primarily in Africa, where it operates as an end-to-end service and CRM system. The product unifies customer data, manages service desk workflows, and processes feedback at scale.

Positioning

The core problem was coherence: one product sold into banking floors, hospital triage, and restaurant front-of-house, in markets across 15+ countries, that still had to read as a single platform. The symbolic anchor came from the continent itself — The Great African Migration, the largest coordinated movement of life on Earth — reframed as a metaphor for what Marezi orchestrates: millions of people in motion across branches and borders, each one expecting to be seen and served. I set the positioning first — "every customer, every touchpoint, one platform" — and let it govern the language on every page, so a bank's IT lead and a restaurant owner both recognised their own problem in the same product.

Scope

The full website — homepage, appointment booking engine, and self-service kiosk, plus seven solution pages and a set of industry pages. Full user journey copy from landing to conversion, structured for a regulated, multi-vertical context and built page by page in Figma alongside the designer.

Page architecture

Rather than a copy pass over a fixed layout, the site was structured as I wrote it — page order, section logic, and internal linking treated as part of the message:

  • Homepage carrying the platform positioning and the four-verb product story — Queue, Serve, Listen, Improve.
  • Seven solution pages, one per capability, each written around a decision the buyer is actually trying to make.
  • Industry pages that reframe the same platform for banking, healthcare, and hospitality without repeating the homepage.
  • Conversion surfaces — booking engine and self-service kiosk — written for the end user standing in a queue, not the buyer.

Approach

AI-assisted research and brief analysis informed the information architecture before a word was written. Copy was structured around user decision points — not product features — with brand voice maintained across the site and adapted to the realities of banking floor, hospital triage, and restaurant front-of-house operations.

Process

Built directly in Figma with the designer, page by page. Positioning and copy were resolved in the same file as the layout, so structure and language moved together instead of copy being poured into a finished design — the reason the site reads as one product rather than a stack of templates.

Deliverable

Production-ready web copy; implemented live.
