Marezi
AI-Driven Web Copywriting

Full website copy for Marezi (formerly Q-SYS), a customer experience platform by Riana Group serving banks, hospitals, and restaurants across Africa — written and structured using an AI-assisted research and drafting workflow.

Client Marezi · Riana Group
Surfaces Homepage · Booking · Kiosk
Status Live

Context

Marezi (formerly Q-SYS) is an intelligent, data-driven customer experience platform developed by the Riana Group. It is deployed across high-volume environments — banks, hospitals, restaurants — primarily in Africa, where it operates as an end-to-end service and CRM system. The product unifies customer data, manages service desk workflows, and processes feedback at scale.

Scope

Homepage, appointment booking engine page, and self-service kiosk page. Full user journey copy from landing to conversion, written for a regulated, multi-vertical context that had to read as one product across very different industries.

Approach

AI-assisted research and brief analysis informed the information architecture before a word was written. Copy was structured around user decision points — not product features — with brand voice maintained across all three pages and adapted to the realities of banking floor, hospital triage, and restaurant front-of-house operations.

Deliverable

Production-ready web copy; implemented live.
