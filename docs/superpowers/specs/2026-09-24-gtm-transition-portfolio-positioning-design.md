The portfolio will separate three layers that must not be conflated:

1. **Current professional identity:** B2B Content & Marketing Operations.
2. **Demonstrated functional work:** content strategy, demand generation, SEO/GEO/AEO, outbound support, campaign execution, analytics, editorial governance, and AI-assisted workflows.
